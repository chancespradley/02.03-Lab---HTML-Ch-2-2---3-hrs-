Boise

Location

Boise is the capital and most populous city of the state of Idaho, it is located in the Treasure Valley in the southwestern part of the state, right along the Boise River.

About

History

Boise, or Fort Boise, was established in 1834 by fur traders along the Oregon Trail. It was officially incorporated as a city under the name "Boise City" in 1864.

Culture

The city is known for its vibrant arts scene, outdoor recreational opportunities, and friendly community. It's nick-named the City of Trees for its greenery in the otherwise desert landscape.

Education

Boise is home to Boise State University a public research university known for its strong programs in business, engineering, and health sciences.

Things To Do

  • Snowboarding at Bogus Basin Mountain Recreation Area
  • Exploring the Boise River Greenbelt or floating the river
  • Attending a Boise State Broncos football game at Albertsons Stadium
